# Privacy Policy — Docket

**Last updated: 24 July 2026**

Docket ("the App") is a Shopify app that helps merchants create purchase
orders, send them to suppliers, and receive stock into their Shopify
inventory. This policy explains what the App stores, why, and for how long.

The App is provided by **Younes Alaoui Lachgar**, Jesionowa 66/7, 50-504
Wrocław, Poland ("we", "us").

---

## 1. What we do not collect

**The App does not collect, store, or process any personal data belonging to
your customers.**

We do not request access to customer records, orders, checkout data, or
marketing information. The App's Shopify permissions cover products,
inventory, and locations only. If you review the App's access scopes in your
Shopify admin, you will see no customer or order permissions listed.

---

## 2. What we store

### 2.1 Data you enter

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supplier name, email, phone, postal address | Addressing and sending purchase orders |
| Supplier currency and document language | Generating purchase order documents |
| Supplier notes | Your own reference; visible only to you |
| Supplier product codes, prices, lead times, reorder points | Pricing purchase orders correctly per supplier, and flagging low stock |
| Purchase orders, line items, quantities, costs, expected delivery dates | The core function of the App |
| Receiving records and delivery notes | Tracking what physically arrived |

Supplier contact details are business contact information for companies you
buy from. If a supplier is a sole trader, this information may constitute
personal data under the GDPR, and is processed on the basis of our legitimate
interest in providing the service you have asked for.

### 2.2 Data from Shopify

To function, the App reads the following from your store and stores copies
where noted:

- **Product and variant details** (title, description, SKU, barcode, cost,
  image) — copied onto purchase order lines so that an order remains an
  accurate record even if a product is later renamed or deleted in Shopify.
- **Location names and IDs** — to record where stock should be received.
- **Inventory quantities** — read at the moment of receiving and when
  checking for low stock, and written back to reflect stock you have received.

### 2.3 Technical data

- Your store's `.myshopify.com` domain and Shopify access token, so the App
  can connect to your store.
- Standard server logs, which may include IP addresses and timestamps,
  retained for security and troubleshooting for **30 days**.

## 6. Retention and deletion

- Data is retained for as long as the App is installed on your store.
- When you uninstall the App, Shopify sends us a shop data erasure request
  approximately **48 hours** later. On receiving it, we delete all suppliers,
  supplier catalogues, purchase orders, receiving records, and session data
  associated with your store.
- You can request deletion at any time by emailing
  **alaoui.youness@gmail.com**.

**You can export all of your purchase order data to CSV at any time, on every
plan, including the free plan.** This is a deliberate design decision: your
records should never be locked inside our app.

---

## 7. Privacy law compliance requests

As required of all Shopify apps, we respond to the mandatory data request,
customer redaction, and shop redaction webhooks. Because the App holds no
customer data, customer data requests and customer redaction requests return
no records. Shop redaction erases everything associated with your store as
described above.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct,
export, delete, or restrict processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint
with a supervisory authority. In Poland, the supervisory authority is the
Personal Data Protection Office (Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych).

To exercise any of these rights, contact **alaoui.youness@gmail.com** and we
will respond within **30 days**.

## 9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on
access-controlled infrastructure. Shopify access tokens are stored
server-side and never exposed to the browser. No security measure is
absolute, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
